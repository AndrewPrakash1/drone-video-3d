Brighton Beach drone survey
Source: https://github.com/pierotofy/drone_dataset_brighton_beach
Author: Piero Toffanin, 2017
License: BSD 2-Clause
18 DJI nadir photos with GPS EXIF, Lake Superior shoreline, Duluth, Minnesota.
flight.mp4 is a 1280x720, 0.5 fps encoding of those frames in filename order.
telemetry.csv is the GPS latitude, longitude, and altitude read from each photo.
